Installation & Setup Guide

Everything you need to install Quirk Notes on iPad, Android, or in your browser, and set up local or cloud AI study tools.

Install on Android

Compatible with 64-bit devices on Android 8 or later (tablets and phones).

  1. Download Quirk-Notes.apk directly onto your Android device.
  2. Open the downloaded APK. If prompted by Android, tap Settings and allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then launch Quirk Notes from your home screen or app drawer.

To update in the future, simply install the newer APK over the existing one. Your notebooks and notes will be preserved.

Install on iPad (iPadOS 17+)

Requires iPadOS 17 or later, an Apple Pencil or capacitive stylus, and a Mac or PC for initial sideloading.

  1. Download Quirk-Notes.ipa onto your computer.
  2. Install Sideloadly (Mac or Windows) or AltStore.
  3. Connect your iPad via USB cable to your computer.
  4. Drag Quirk-Notes.ipa into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID credentials, and click Start.
  5. On your iPad, navigate to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management and tap Trust on your developer certificate.

Note: With a free Apple ID, personal developer certificates renew every 7 days via Wi-Fi or cable.

Use in Browser (PWA)

For Chromebooks, desktop PCs, or instant handwriting on any tablet without installation.

  1. Open the Quirk Notes Web Edition.
  2. In Safari (iPad), tap Share and select Add to Home Screen. In Chrome/Brave, tap the install icon in the address bar.
  3. The web app launches full-screen and works 100% offline via service worker caching.

Configuring AI Study Tools

Quirk Notes gives you two private options for study flashcards, summaries, and quizzes:

Option 1: Free NVIDIA NIM Online Key (Fastest & Highest Quality)

  1. Visit build.nvidia.com and sign up for a free developer account.
  2. Generate a free personal API key (includes 1,000 free monthly LLM calls).
  3. Open Quirk Notes, go to Settings › Online AI, and paste your key.

Option 2: 100% Offline On-Device Model (Zero Internet Needed)

  1. In Quirk Notes, open Settings › On-Device AI.
  2. Tap Download Local Model (1.1 GB one-time download: quantized Qwen 1.7B).
  3. Once downloaded, all note summaries, quizzes, and flashcards run directly on your iPad or Android processor without a single packet leaving your device.
